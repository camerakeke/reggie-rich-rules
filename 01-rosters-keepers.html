Roster Size

2026 roster expansion.

2026 UPDATE

Three roster spots will be added before the draft, increasing roster size from 22 to 25 players.

This gives playoff teams more flexibility once waivers close and keeps non-playoff teams participating in the Playoff Competition from being too restricted.

Keeper Rules

Core keeper limits, Franchise designation, injury/suspension rules and dropped-player eligibility.

Core Keeper Rules

Each team may keep a maximum of 5 players total:

  • 4 offensive players
  • 1 defensive player or kicker

The 5-player limit includes Franchise Players and players receiving an extra keeper year through Taxi Squad or injury/suspension rules.

Only players drafted in Round 5 or later are keeper-eligible under normal circumstances. Undrafted players acquired through waivers or free agency are also keeper-eligible.

A player may be kept for 3 seasons under normal keeper status.

Franchise Player Rule

A player may be kept for 3 seasons under normal keeper status. After that, the player may be designated as the team’s Franchise Player and kept year-to-year as long as the team continues to use its one Franchise designation on that player.

Each team may have only 1 Franchise Player at a time, and that player still counts toward the 5-player keeper limit.

Franchise status follows the player if traded. Nothing resets because of a trade.

Keeper Injury / Suspension Extra-Year Rule

2026 UPDATE

A keeper receives an extra year of keeper eligibility only if both conditions are met:

  • The player is placed on NFL Injured Reserve or is suspended by the end of Week 10.
  • The player remains unavailable through the end of Week 14.

A player first placed on IR or first suspended after Week 10 does not qualify, even if the absence later becomes season-ending.

This replaces the older method of counting how many NFL games a player missed or played.

Dropped Player Keeper Eligibility

Rule: If a player is dropped and picked up by a different team, the player’s keeper-year clock resets with the new team.

Round 1–4 Players: Players drafted in Rounds 1–4 are not keeper-eligible unless they are dropped and later picked up by a different team. If that happens, they become keeper-eligible and start a new keeper-year clock with the new team.

Season-Ending IR Exception: If the player is on season-ending IR, they do not become keeper-eligible when picked up.

GM Responsibility: Before dropping a Round 1–4 player because of injury, the GM must confirm that the player’s IR status is season-ending.

Round 5+ and Undrafted Players: Players drafted in Round 5 or later, and undrafted players, remain keeper-eligible after being dropped and picked up by another team. Their keeper-year clock resets with the new team.

Original Team Reacquires Player: If the original team drops and later reacquires the player, the keeper-year clock does not reset.

Traded Player Keeper Eligibility

If a keeper-eligible player is traded, all keeper status follows the player.

Nothing resets because of a trade, including the player’s keeper year, Franchise status and existing keeper eligibility.
