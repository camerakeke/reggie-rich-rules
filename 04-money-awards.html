Champion Trophy Option

The league sets aside up to $80 for the champion’s trophy.

The champion may choose either:

  • A trophy valued at $80 or less, or
  • Add the $80 trophy allowance to their championship winnings and purchase their own trophy.

If the champion chooses a trophy costing more than $80, the champion pays the difference.

Total league money accounted for: $2,400.

2026 Leftover Late Fee Awards

$375 in leftover 2025 late fees applied as 2027 league-fee credits.

For the 2026 season, the $375 in leftover late fees will be used as award credits toward each winning GM’s 2027 league fee. These awards will not be paid out in cash; the credited amount will be added to the GM’s LeagueSafe balance for their 2027 league fees.

Player Award Eligibility Rule

To qualify for any player-based award, a player must be on a fantasy roster by the end of Week 10 and must remain on a fantasy roster through the end of Week 14.

If the winning player is not on a fantasy roster at the award cutoff, that award will not be paid out and the prize money will roll over to the following season’s award pool.
