Taxi Squad Rules

Rookie/second-year Taxi Squad eligibility and extra-year requirements.

Eligibility

Only rookies and second-year players are eligible for the Taxi Squad.

A player may only be added to the Taxi Squad once per season. Once removed, that player cannot be placed back on Taxi that season.

GMs should be strategic when using Taxi Squad spots for players they may want to activate later.

Extra-Year Keeper Eligibility

2026 UPDATE

To receive the extra year of keeper eligibility, a rookie or second-year player must:

  • Be placed on the Taxi Squad by the end of Week 14.
  • Have started no more than 3 fantasy games for their current team.
  • Remain on the Taxi Squad through the end of the fantasy season, including the playoffs.

A player may start up to 3 fantasy games and still retain the extra year. The player loses the extra year upon the 4th fantasy start for that team.

Players Acquired by Trade

2026 UPDATE

Fantasy starts made for the previous team do not count against Taxi Squad eligibility with the new team.

Only starts made for the current team count toward the 3-start limit.

Waiver Wire Players

2026 UPDATE

A rookie or second-year player acquired through waivers may qualify for the extra keeper year as long as the player meets all Taxi Squad requirements and is placed on Taxi by the end of Week 14.

If a team drops and later reacquires a player, that player’s previous starts with that same team still count.

Playoff Taxi Squad Rules

2026 UPDATE

Once the fantasy playoffs begin, players may not be added to the Taxi Squad.

Players may only be removed from the Taxi Squad during the playoffs.

If a player is removed from the Taxi Squad at any point during the playoffs, including being activated for a playoff lineup, that player will not receive the extra year of keeper eligibility.

Simple Eligibility Check for GMs

  • Rookie or second-year player?
  • Placed on Taxi by the end of Week 14?
  • Still on Taxi at the end of the fantasy season?
  • 3 or fewer fantasy starts for the current team?

If all four are yes, the player receives the extra keeper year.

Trade Rules

Trade processing, Round 1–4 restrictions, backend agreements and anti-collusion rules.

General Trade Review

Trades will process without commissioner review unless they violate league rules, involve an undisclosed backend agreement, or raise a clear collusion concern.

The commissioner may step in, rework, reverse, or veto a trade if one of those issues applies.

Players Drafted in Rounds 1–4 — Draft Pick Trade Rule

If a player drafted in Rounds 1–4 is traded for draft picks only, the return must include a draft pick from the same round the player was originally drafted.

Exception: If another player drafted in that same round is included in the trade, the same-round draft-pick requirement does not apply.

This rule applies regardless of the size of the trade package.

Backend Trade Rule

Any trade that includes a side agreement, future agreement, conditional return, or other arrangement outside the posted trade must be documented.

The agreement may either be disclosed to the commissioner, or put in writing, dated, and given to a third party chosen by the teams involved.

If the full backend agreement cannot be verified, the trade may be reworked, reversed, or vetoed.

Same-Team Trade-Back Rule

A player traded away cannot be traded back to the same team during the same season.
