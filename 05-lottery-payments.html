Draft Lottery Points

Lottery standings determine the number of balls each team receives for draft order.

Whatever place you finish in the Lottery Ticket Point standings will determine the number of lottery balls you receive for the draft order.

The fewer lottery balls you have, the better your chance of receiving a higher draft pick.

Example: 2nd place = 2 balls · 12th place = 12 balls

Lottery points are accumulated through all 18 weeks.

Negative Points

  • Weekly Low Score: -1
  • Score 99 points or lower: -1
  • Lowest Scoring Team for the Season: -3
  • Non-Playoff Team: -2
  • Team with the Worst Record: -3
  • Player scores negative fantasy points: -1
  • Individual player scores -15 or lower: -3 total

The -15-or-lower penalty is a flat -3 and does not increase for a lower score.

Positive Points

  • First 6 people to pay league fee: +1
  • Weekly High Score: +1
  • Kicker scores 20+: +1
  • Defensive Player scores 20+: +1
  • Individual player scores 50+: +1
  • Individual player scores 75+: +3
  • Team scores 200+: +1
  • Playoff Team that is not a Division Winner: +2
  • Secure Bye Week: +2
  • Division Winner: +3
  • Best Record: +3
  • Highest Scoring Team for the Season: +3
  • Win Playoff Scoring Competition: +2
  • Advance a round in the Playoff Scoring Competition: +1
  • Win a Playoff Game in the first 2 rounds: +2
  • Runner-Up: +3
  • Champion: +5
The Draft Lottery “First 6 to pay” +1 is separate from the Deposit Deadline reward rules.

Deposit Deadline Rule

League deposit, reward eligibility and final payment deadlines.

All league deposits must be paid by July 1st at 8:00 PM.

Any team that does not pay their deposit by the July 1st deadline will be subject to a league penalty. The deposit penalty will be revealed after the July 1st deadline.

To be eligible for any league reward or bonus connected to missed-deposit penalties, teams must pay their deposit by June 19th.

Teams that pay after June 19th but on or before July 1st at 8:00 PM will avoid the late-deposit penalty, but they will not be eligible for any reward or bonus connected to missed-deposit penalties.

Final league fees are due by August 28th at 8:00 PM.

Any team that does not pay their final league fees by the August 28th deadline will be subject to a separate league penalty.

This rule is in place to encourage early payment, keep the league organized, avoid last-minute issues before the season, and prevent issues with tracking down payments at the end of the year. It is also about fairness to the GMs who pay their dues on time. We all want a fair, seamless, smooth fantasy season, and that includes winners being paid out.

Beginning in 2027, clear league payment due dates will be announced ahead of time.
