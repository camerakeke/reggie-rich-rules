Before Week 1Taxi Season Lock: During the preseason, the Taxi return restriction stays at 0 days so GMs may freely move eligible rookies and second-year players in and out while setting their roster. Before the regular season begins, the commissioner changes the return restriction back to 365 days. From that point forward, once a player is promoted off Taxi, that player cannot be placed back on Taxi that season.
WeeklyFree agents are placed on waivers Monday at 6:00 PM PT. Blind-bid waivers process Tuesday at 8:00 PM PT.
November 22 — 9:45 AM PT (Week 11)Trade deadline. No trades are allowed after this time.
Before Week 15Taxi Playoff Setting: Commissioner changes Taxi eligibility to No Players. This prevents any new players from being placed on Taxi during the playoffs while still allowing each GM to promote their own players already on Taxi if needed. The purpose is to keep playoff roster management hands-off for the commissioner.
Before Week 15Playoff NO ADDS Test: Commissioner changes the weekly waiver-add limit from 7 to 0. This is being tested during the 2026 season to see whether MFL will block new waiver/free-agent additions while still allowing GMs to make necessary drops themselves, including drops needed to activate a player from Taxi or IR. The broader all add/drop transaction limits will not be changed.
Week 15Fantasy playoffs and the league-wide Playoff Competition begin.
Week 18Playoff Competition Final — highest-scoring team among the final 3 wins the pot.
Calendar Note: The scheduled MFL setting changes above are documented in advance so all GMs know what will change, when it will change, and why.
