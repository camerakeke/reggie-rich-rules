Playoffs, Tiebreakers & Seeding

Division tiebreakers, wildcard tiebreakers and 2026 playoff seeding.

Division Winner Tiebreakers — 2 Teams

  1. Head-to-Head
  2. Division Record
  3. Points

Division Winner Tiebreakers — 3 or More Teams

  1. Head-to-Head
  2. Division Record
  3. Points

If two teams remain tied after one or more teams are eliminated during any step, the tiebreaker reverts to Step 1 of the two-team format.

Wildcard Tiebreakers

  1. Standings / Overall Record
  2. Head-to-Head
  3. Points Scored

If the tied teams are from the same division, use the Division Winner Tiebreakers.

Three-Team Exception: If 3 teams from different divisions are tied and are 1–1 against each other, the tiebreaker goes to Points Scored.

Playoff Seeding

2026 MAJOR UPDATE
  • Playoff seeding is based on overall record, regardless of division finish.
  • The team with the best overall record receives the #1 seed.
  • Division winners are not automatically guaranteed the top two seeds.
  • A Division Winner cannot be seeded lower than #4.
  • If two teams are tied in overall record, Head-to-Head is the first tiebreaker.
  • If Head-to-Head does not break the tie, use Division Record if applicable, then Points Scored.
  • Seeds 1–7 are determined by overall record and the approved tiebreakers, with the #4 minimum seed protection for Division Winners.
  • In the 2nd round, the highest remaining seed plays the lowest remaining seed.

Previously: Division winners were guaranteed the top two seeds.

Playoff Competition Rules

Separate league-wide playoff scoring competition beginning in Week 15.

The Playoff Competition begins in Week 15.

Every team will continue to set a lineup, including teams that have been eliminated from the regular fantasy playoffs.

Each week, the 3 teams with the lowest scores will be eliminated.

  • Week 15: 12 teams → 9 teams
  • Week 16: 9 teams → 6 teams
  • Week 17: 6 teams → 3 teams
  • Week 18: Final 3 teams

The highest-scoring team among the final 3 in Week 18 wins the pot.
